Dhaara: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Dhaara is a practice app for the All India Bar Examination (AIBE) and judiciary exams. This page explains what information Dhaara collects, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

Sign-up details you enter: name, mobile number, optional email, the exam you are preparing for, your state, attempt year and your WhatsApp/SMS preference.

Study progress: XP, answers, mock scores, daily activity counts, chosen senior advocate and similar progress inside the app.

Sign-in: Dhaara creates an anonymous account on your device. If you choose "Sign in with Google", we receive your Google account's email address and a user ID so your progress follows you to other devices.

Photo: your avatar photo stays on your device. If you choose to show it on the leaderboard, a small 40×40 pixel copy is stored with your leaderboard entry.

How we use it

To run your account and save your progress; to show the leaderboard (name and surname, state, XP, scores and, if you allow it, your small photo); to understand overall use of the app; and, if you opted in, to send you practice questions and course updates on WhatsApp or SMS.

What we don't do

We don't sell your information. We don't show advertising. Your mobile number and email are never shown to other students.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Google Cloud) and the app is served by GitHub Pages. These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy terms.

Your choices

You can turn off WhatsApp/SMS updates, hide your photo from the leaderboard, or ask us to delete your account and all your data by writing to the contact address below. We will act on deletion requests within 30 days.

Children

Dhaara is meant for law students and graduates. It is not directed at children under 18.

Disclaimer

Dhaara is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, the Bar Council of India or any court. Summaries of judgments are educational; always read the official judgment.
